Title and work-count context:
Schubert D.780 / Op.94 contains six pieces; D.935 / Op.142 contains four Impromptus.
The title Moments musicaux was supplied by Schubert's publisher.
No.3 first appeared in 1823 as Air russe; the collected set appeared in 1828.
Rachmaninoff Op.16 (1896) also contains six original piano pieces.
A matching title and six-piece format do not establish a number-by-number correspondence
or a composer-declared homage. Do not label the Rachmaninoff score as a Park encore.
