PianoMoDa · Schubert D.946 edition notes · 2026-10-19

Work: Drei Klavierstücke, D.946 / 《세 개의 피아노 소품》
1. Allegro assai; 2. Allegretto; 3. Allegro.

Performance: 2026-10-16, JongHai Park, Seoul Arts Center IBK Chamber Hall.
The concert attendee confirmed that No.1 followed Schubert's autograph deletion,
matching the A-B-A construction in the score already hosted on this site.
This is a performance record; the site PDF is a printed reference score.

G. Henle Verlag, HN 66, Preface (Summer 1976 / spring 1996):
https://www.henle.de/media/de/9d/88/1690886063/0066-1690886063-sync.pdf
The preface discusses following the first edition for corrections and identifies
the deleted passage specifically in the first piece. Its bar numbering is 274-326.

Henle's discussion of No.1, 2013-07-22:
https://blog.henle.de/en/2013/07/22/schubert-deletes-brahms-restores-on-the-first-of-the-three-posthumous-piano-pieces-impromptus-d-946-by-franz-schubert/
The autograph deletes the second contrasting episode. The 1868 posthumous first
edition includes it. The longer layout is A-B-A-C-A; the deletion-based layout is
A-B-A. The passage is Schubert's composition, not newly composed music by Brahms.
Use 'the posthumous first edition reinstated it' rather than assigning sole
responsibility for that publication decision to Brahms.

Henle complete-edition contents:
https://www.henle.de/us/3-Piano-Pieces-Impromptus-op.-post.-D-946/HN-66
All three pieces are listed. The comparison upload contained only No.1.
